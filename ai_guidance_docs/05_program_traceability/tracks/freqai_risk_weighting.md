# Track - FreqAI Risk and Weighting

## 1. Boundary

This track preserves trader-readable FreqAI hypotheses, controls, historical model conclusions, and the planned dynamic risk/weighting role. Historical/live source acquisition belongs to the data tracks. Objective 02b's FreqAI reaction-zone and event-hierarchy work is historical; the current primary stage is the bounded comparative PAPER trial under Objective 03.

Objective 02b's FreqAI reaction-zone and event-hierarchy work is historical and parked; earlier FreqAI/context experiments remain leads with their own coverage limits. Unrestricted every-candle direction and promotion of historical FreqAI models remain unapproved. Any current paper heuristics are governed separately by Objective 03 and do not validate a FreqAI model or a canonical master integration.

## 2. Intended role

FreqAI should estimate named conditional risks or opportunities—such as follow-through, drawdown, breakdown continuation, absorption, volatility expansion, or source-specific invalidation—and apply a bounded modifier to deterministic entry/exit evidence.

It should influence enter/skip, size, later leverage, add permission, tighten/reduce, partial, runner, or exit decisions. It should not replace the trader-readable entry family with an opaque prediction.

## 3. Current state

- Historical event-scoped price/structure labels were more promising than generic next-return labels in some bounded comparisons; this is not promotion-grade evidence.
- Orderbook wall distance, spread fragility, support removal, and failure states look more useful as precise caution/invalidation evidence than broad entry filters.
- Live media/context produced several useful AUC lifts and cross-pair shapes, but the temporal history remains short.
- Strict live media plus orderbook overlap can leave only tens or hundreds of rows.
- Historical aggregate GDELT leads are exploratory and are not equivalent to article/story-aware live media.
- Earlier false-clean coverage, timestamp, taxonomy, eligibility, and ablation issues invalidate unqualified reuse of some old results.
- The 10 September whole-episode guardrail recheck invalidated older promotion/model interpretations affected by episode duplication. Preserve this correction when retaining earlier leads.
- Activity/readiness is not direction or causation; downstream volume is not a competing news cause merely because it predicts continuation. Short, uneven coverage and whole-event holdouts constrain interpretation; parked leads are not universal disproof.
- Objective 02b's event hierarchy remains historical research, not an active FreqAI queue. No durable profitability or trading promotion has been established.

## 5. Historical conclusions and leads

These are retained research leads, not current acceptance findings. Apply the 10 September audit and source-coverage caveats before interpreting any older headline or model row.

### Where the investigations ended

- Generic-exit/early-context comparisons and the 3Ki sweep remain historical, unpromoted leads.
- Level/cluster work identified candidate busy reaction locations; that does not establish standalone direction.
- CPI/FOMC release-activity observations remain short-window leads; directional use requires true expectations plus market confirmation.
- Media/activity and recent-volume signals are conditional readiness/continuation context, not evidence against a news cause.
- Fragmented orderbook/live overlap and the whole-episode audit limit reuse; no retained result establishes durable profitability.

See `../../04_results/results_recent_summary.md`, `../../04_results/context_research_ledger.md`, and `../../../user_data/research_news_data/context_features/reports/general_exit_freqai_results_ledger.csv` for the bounded summaries and audit trail; the 3Ki sweep source is `../../../user_data/research_news_data/context_features/3ki_freqai_2020_2022/tools/run_ta_confluence_freqai_sweep.py`.

### Promising leads (unconfirmed)

- Trader-readable event labels with price-only controls.
- Live topic-risk, media-activity, and source-burst/volume shapes.
- Orderbook as exact invalidation/caution evidence.
- Conditional rather than universal use of VP/context features.

### Weak or unstable

- Generic next-return targets and broad context-only/fakeout labels.
- Broad orderbook filters and generic risk-size reducers.
- Model results with many more features than effective rows.
- Conclusions derived from zero-filled, stale, or incorrectly eligible sources.

### Not yet established

- Promotion-grade temporal stability.
- A reliable combined live-media/orderbook/structure risk model.
- A calibrated dynamic multiplier suitable for the final strategy.

### Conditional future tests

- Revalidate only the strongest labels across time and pairs.
